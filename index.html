How it works

From quote to vault in four steps

  1. 01

    Quote

    Tell us the metal, form, quantity and destination. We price against the live spot benchmark.

  2. 02

    Onboard

    A fast KYC/AML onboarding for your entity, with a dedicated relationship manager.

  3. 03

    Bars are assayed, serial-numbered and allocated to you, with certificates attached.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Insured air or sea transport to your vault, refinery or bank — tracked end to end.
